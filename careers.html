Simple, human, no portals.

No account creation, no automated resume black holes, and no ten-round interview gauntlets. Just send a clear email to real people.

01

Send us an email

Write to careers@gatito.tech with the role title in the subject line — or use "General Application" if you're pitching your own way to help.

02

Show what you've built

Share links to work you're proud of — GitHub repos, live apps, designs, writing, or community projects. A resume is welcome, but real work and clear thinking matter most.

03

Talk like a human

We read every application ourselves. If there's a potential match, we'll reply directly to set up a relaxed conversation and talk through next steps.

What to include in your email

Keep it short, plain, and honest. Here's what helps us get to know you:

  • The role you're applying for (or the kind of work you'd love to do here).
  • A brief intro — who you are and why privacy-first, independent software matters to you.
  • Links to your work — GitHub, portfolio, side projects, or a resume/CV if you have one.
  • Timezone & availability — whether you're looking for full-time, part-time, or contract work.
